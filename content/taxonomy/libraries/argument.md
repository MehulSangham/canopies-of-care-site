# Argument structure specification (Toulmin)

Reference for how a claim node on the project map should be specified. The public x-ray Argument tab is a view of these fields on the claim attached to the page.

## Elements

- **Claim** — the assertion, stated in one or two sentences. Not a slogan. Not “this page argues.”
- **Grounds** — what must be shown. Discrete, checkable facts or documented patterns. One ground per bullet.
- **Warrant** — the inferential bridge: why those grounds, if true, entitle the claim. This is the logic, not more evidence.
- **Backing** — (optional) the authority for the warrant: a statute, a scholarly consensus, a primary quotation.
- **Qualifier** — strength and limits. What the claim does *not* say. Disputed figures stay in the qualifier.
- **Rebuttal** — the strongest honest objection, in the objector’s terms.
- **Answer** — why the rebuttal does not sink the claim.

## Roles of a passage relative to a claim

A paragraph may:

- **Advance the reframe** — instantiate the claim or a reframe metaphor.
- **Describe the dominant account** — show the prevailing story (container, accounting, deservingness) so it can be located. Do not “correct” this language into showing-up language.
- **Aim at a catalytic community** — a hinge beat (crisis kitchen, lodge, testimony) meant to move someone at the edge of the discourse window.

## Spine (project claims)

The archive argument is a chain. If all hold, the tradition is continuous.

**A — Founding tradition:** A0 elder reciprocity; A1 1787 parallel founding; A2 excluded communities as civic infrastructure; A3 fraternal scale (one in three men).
**B — Survival through American identity:** B1 New Deal displacement; B2 civil rights care; B3 Panther programs and co-option; B4 AIDS networks; B5 maternal care.
**C — Not only in-group:** C1 crisis expansion; C2 labour across ethnic lines; C3 institutions built for everyone; C4 boundedness reflected exclusion, not principle.
**D — Displacement and contest:** D1 legal/institutional undermining; D2 competing tradition of “American”; D3 Project 2025 as culmination; D4 the archive contests that displacement.

## Rules for the assistant

- Never invent grounds. Cite the citation file, page footnotes, or a fetched source.
- Prefer attaching an existing claim node to creating a new one.
- A new claim node must include claim + grounds + warrant at minimum.
- Do not attach *advances the reframe* to a paragraph that activates Eligibility, Compliance, or Deserving as its main frame.
