# Conceptual metaphor library

Reference inventory for the assistant. These are not project nodes until they are added to the argument map. Source: Phase 1 linguistic reframe (Lakoff & Johnson; Lakoff *Moral Politics*; archive reframe architecture).

## Exclusionary system (dominant account)

### NATION IS CONTAINER
The nation is a bounded vessel. Borders are walls; citizens are inside; non-citizens are outside. Care is for contents. Written into law as “aliens within the Nation’s borders.” Makes exclusion look like geography.

### MORALITY IS ACCOUNTING
Moral worth is a balance sheet. Credits for contribution, debits for need. “Paid his dues,” “public charge.” Makes care transactional: you withdraw only after you pay in.

### MORAL STRENGTH
Moral character is physical strength. Receiving help without earning it is weakness. Self-reliance is strength. Justifies withholding care as character-building.

### CARE IS EXCHANGE
Care is a commodity. You must pay before you receive. Receiving without paying is freeloading.

### CARE IS A SCARCE RESOURCE
Care is finite and zero-sum. More for them is less for us. Exclusion reads as rationing, not a political choice.

### NATION IS A FAMILY (Strict Father)
The nation is a family; government is the father; citizens are children who must learn discipline. Conditional care is moral education.

### CARE IS CHARITY
Care flows one way, from a benefactor to a beneficiary. The receiver is grateful; the giver is generous. Reciprocal obligation is invisible.

### BELONGING IS CATEGORY
You belong by what you are (citizen, legal, deserving), not by what you do.

## Reframe system (the account we promulgate)

### COMMUNITY IS FABRIC
Community is woven, not bounded. No inside/outside. Weakness in the weave requires more weaving, not cutting threads. Counters NATION IS CONTAINER.

### BELONGING IS SHOWING UP
You belong by what you do — showing up, contributing care — not by status. Counters category-based belonging.

### HELP IS SUPPORT
Care is structural reinforcement. Helping strengthens the whole. Counters CARE IS EXCHANGE.

### CARE IS BUILDING
Care work produces infrastructure (a fund, a lodge, a kitchen). It creates rather than depletes. Counters CARE IS A SCARCE RESOURCE.

### BELONGING IS LOCATION
Presence, not category. Being here is belonging here. Counters NATION IS CONTAINER.

### SOLIDARITY IS STANDING TOGETHER
Proximity and shared practice are commitment. “Americans show up for each other.”

### CARE IS NURTURE
Care is growth-oriented, not transactional. The question is what people need to flourish, not who has earned a withdrawal.

### COMMUNITY IS KINSHIP (k'é)
Obligation is the substance of relation. You are related to the people and places you are responsible to.

## Contested

### NATION IS PERSON
Dominant in some civic-nationalist discourse. Do not attack the personification. Change the entailment: a strong nation takes care of its own — all of its own.
