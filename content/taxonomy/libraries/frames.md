# Semantic frame library

Reference inventory for the assistant. FrameNet-style frames that organise care and belonging talk. Naming a frame in prose can activate it — the reframe strategy is to foreground some and avoid naming others even to argue against them.

## Frames to foreground (reframe)

### Collaboration
Partners work together toward a shared outcome. No benefactor. Roles are reciprocal.

### Assistance
A helper acts so that a benefited party is better off. Can slide into charity; keep it paired with Collaboration so the helped party is also a helper elsewhere.

### Building
An agent creates a structure from components. Care work as construction of shared infrastructure.

### Inclusion_scenario
An includer brings someone into a group through practice, not through a test of desert.

### Reciprocity
Giving creates obligation; obligation is the web. Standing is measured by what circulates.

## Frames to avoid naming (dominant / incumbent)

### Compliance
A party must satisfy a rule to remain in good standing. Activates gating even when criticised.

### Eligibility
Access depends on meeting criteria. The question “who qualifies?” crowds out “who showed up?”

### Deserving
Moral desert as the hinge of help. Close to Eligibility; more moralised.

### Charity
One-way gift from surplus. Erases dues, lodges, and reciprocal funds.

### Security / Container
The group must be protected at its boundary. Pairs with NATION IS CONTAINER.

## Catalytic frames (edge of the discourse window)

### Crisis_response
Neighbours organise when the official system lags. Opens the reframe without a partisan argument: barn-raising, kitchen, sandbag line.

### Testimony
Lived practice spoken as fact, not as advocacy. Veterans, first responders, disaster crews. The listening model treats these communities as catalytic.

### Tradition
A practice is old and repeated. “We always have.” Moves catalytic audiences toward civic identity without activating Eligibility.
