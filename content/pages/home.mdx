---
title: "Canopies of Care"
subtitle: "A History of Mutual Aid as American Civic Tradition"
---

In 1787, two things happened in Philadelphia: the Constitutional Convention wrote the rules for who would be recognised as American, and the Free African Society built the infrastructure for those the rules excluded. Both are founding traditions. Only one is remembered.

This archive recovers the other tradition. It traces mutual aid from 1787 to the present, documenting a continuous practice of care, reciprocity, and collective provision that is as old as the Republic, as widespread as any civic institution, and as invisible as the people who built it.

The history is organised into four sections, each making a set of claims supported by primary and secondary sources. Together, they establish that mutual aid is not a radical departure from American values but a recovery of them.
